Our AI Engineering Process

A disciplined methodology that validates feasibility before committing to full-scale production engineering.

  1. 01 Problem Scoping & Data Audit We analyze your workflows, evaluate existing data quality, and determine whether AI is the optimal and cost-effective approach.
  2. 02 Architecture & Benchmark Design We select the optimal model families, define evaluation metrics (precision, recall, latency, cost per token), and design security guardrails.
  3. 03 Prototype & Empirical Validation We build a fast, verifiable proof-of-concept against your actual data benchmarks to prove efficacy and test edge cases.
  4. 04 Production Engineering We implement scalable microservices, vector storage, caching layers, and database connectors with comprehensive error handling.
  5. 05 Deployment, Monitoring & Observability We deploy to your private cloud or managed infrastructure, monitoring latency, token economics, drift, and accuracy continuously.

Frequently Asked Questions

What custom AI solutions does BRIKCODE develop?

We develop production-grade AI systems including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) engines, customized machine learning models, computer vision pipelines, natural language processing tools, and secure AI APIs integrated with your business software.

How does BRIKCODE ensure client data privacy during AI development?

We implement strict data isolation protocols, zero-data-retention API policies, on-premises or private cloud deployment options, and bilateral non-disclosure agreements before commencing any work.

Can you integrate AI into our existing software stack?

Yes. We build clean API wrappers and event-driven connectors that interface seamlessly with existing SQL/NoSQL databases, enterprise ERPs, CRMs, and web platforms.

How long does a typical custom AI project take?

A focused prototype or validation phase typically takes 2 to 4 weeks. Full production deployment with cloud infrastructure, automated testing, and integrations usually spans 6 to 12 weeks depending on scope.
