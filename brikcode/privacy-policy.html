Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This Privacy Policy details how BRIKCODE collects, protects, and manages data when you interact with our website and software development services.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a consultation, or submit project requirements through our website, we may collect:

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number).
  • Company or organization name and technical specifications.
  • Project details, timelines, architectural requirements, and budget ranges.
  • Technical website usage data (browser type, standard server logs, anonymized analytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to:

  • Respond to your technical inquiries and project scoping requests.
  • Prepare non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and engineering proposals.
  • Deliver agreed custom AI development, SaaS, and software engineering services.
  • Maintain and optimize our website's security, performance, and user experience.

3. Client Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

BRIKCODE treats all proprietary client ideas, codebase access, datasets, and commercial information with absolute confidentiality. We routinely execute bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to architectural reviews or code inspection. We never sell, rent, or trade client data or project information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All web communications are encrypted via HTTPS / TLS.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses minimal, privacy-conscious cookies essential for navigation and aggregated performance analysis. You can configure your browser to reject cookies without affecting fundamental access to our website.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To make a request, contact our data protection team at brikcode07@gmail.com.
